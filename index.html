PLANETS

Earth is our home planet, rich with life, water, and diverse climates. It is the only known planet to support life, thanks to its perfect balance of atmosphere, temperature, and resources.

Mars is a cold, dusty planet known as the "Red Planet" due to its iron-rich soil. Scientists believe it may have once had water and could possibly support life in the future.

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system, a massive gas giant with swirling storms and dozens of moons. Its most famous feature is the Great Red Spot—a giant storm larger than Earth.
